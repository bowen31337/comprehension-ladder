1. This video explains the comprehension ladder. It is a skill that helps a person understand something.
2. The skill has four rungs. Prose, a diagram, a web page, and a video. This video is rung four.
3. You ask a question. The skill reads your words and picks a rung.
4. It also picks a strictness. The default is eighty percent of the way to Simplified Technical English.
5. Strictness sets the sentence limit. At eighty percent, a sentence has twenty five words or fewer.
6. Before it makes anything, the skill writes one outline. Claims, mechanism, hedges, and one real example.
7. Every rung shows that same outline. When you climb, the form changes. The facts stay the same.
8. Rule one. Keep every hedge. If the source says "may be caused by a race condition", the skill keeps the word "may".
9. Rule two. Add no facts. If a diff does not say why, the explanation says so.
10. A linter checks the text. It finds long sentences, semicolons and marketing words. It never flags a hedge.
11. A video needs a voice, a renderer and ffmpeg. The skill uses the first voice it finds.
12. If Manim is not installed, a small renderer draws each frame with numpy and Pillow. This video was made that way.
13. The linter checks form only. It cannot tell if an explanation is true. The outline and the two rules do that job.
14. So pick a rung, keep the facts, and climb when you need more.
